Common questions

How do I correct something I logged?

Open History, select the entry, and use its edit or delete action. Dose, symptom, benefit, and weight entries can be corrected.

Why did my reminder not arrive?

Confirm Dose reminders are enabled in Account & Settings and notifications are allowed in your phone settings. Battery-saving or focus modes can delay notifications.

Can I use HealthStix without internet?

After signing in, core tracking remains local-first so you can log and review available on-device data without a connection. Sign-in, purchases, password recovery, sharing, and cloud sync require internet. When you reconnect, HealthStix+ users can use Sync now to exchange queued changes.

How do I reset or change my password?

Use Forgot Password on the sign-in screen if you cannot sign in. If you are signed in, use Change password under Security in Account & Settings.

Where are my photos stored?

Progress Photos requires HealthStix+; vial reference photos do not. Both stay on the device where you add them. They are not uploaded by cloud sync, included as images in provider reports, or shared with another person.

What happens when I sign out?

Your local data remains protected on that device so it is available when you sign back into the same account. Moving the device to a different account requires confirming that its local data will be erased.

Is HealthStix giving medical advice?

No. HealthStix organizes information you enter and is not a diagnosis, dosing recommendation, or substitute for a qualified healthcare professional.

HealthStix+ questions

What do I get with HealthStix+? HealthStix+

HealthStix+ unlocks unlimited concurrent protocols, full History and Schedule ranges, full Insights and Vial Economics, Progress Photos, provider PDF and CSV exports, multiple household members, read-only sharing, cloud sync across devices, and every theme and Pin Cushion skin.

Why is data missing on another device? HealthStix+

Confirm both devices use the same HealthStix account and both have Enable cloud sync turned on in Account & Settings. Tap Sync now on the device with the latest entry first, then tap Sync now on the other device. Vial and progress photos stay local and do not sync.

What happens if HealthStix+ ends? HealthStix+

Your data is preserved, not deleted. HealthStix keeps the protocol with the most recently logged dose available on the free plan, even if it is paused; other protocols return if you resubscribe. Pause does not switch the retained protocol. Stop is permanent and preserves history; stopping it reveals the next eligible existing protocol, or lets you create one if none remain.

Why does my subscription look different on another device? HealthStix+

One HealthStix+ subscription works across iOS and Android when both devices use the same HealthStix account. Make sure both devices are online and signed into the same email, then close and reopen HealthStix. Use Restore Purchases if the purchasing device still does not recognize an active store subscription.

Can I track more than one person or protocol? HealthStix+

HealthStix+ supports multiple household members and unlimited concurrent protocols. HealthStix keeps each person’s history, schedule, and insights separate.

What can someone see when I share a person? HealthStix+

An accepted share is read-only and shows that person’s protocols and dose history. Symptoms, benefits, notes, body measurements, labs, and photos remain private.

How can I give records to a provider? HealthStix+

In Account & Settings under Your Data, create a provider-ready PDF for a readable report or export CSV files for your own analysis.

Still need help?

Email healthstix@spacecityapps.com. Include what happened, what you expected, your device platform, and the app version shown in Account & Settings. Do not include passwords, verification codes, or sensitive medical details.

HealthStix does not provide medical advice or emergency support. For urgent medical concerns, contact a qualified healthcare professional or your local emergency services.
