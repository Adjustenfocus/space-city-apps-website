Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how HealthStix (“the App,” “we,” “us”) handles information when you use our mobile application. HealthStix is developed and operated by Justen Wilson.

SummaryInformationHow we use itData sharingRetention & deletionYour rightsMedical disclaimerContact

1. Summary

HealthStix helps you track peptide protocols, doses, and progress—including for multiple people in one household, such as a spouse or family member you help manage shots for. Tracking data is stored on your device. If an eligible user enables Cloud sync, structured tracking data is also stored using Supabase, a hosted database provider, so it can sync across that user’s devices. Progress-photo and vial-photo image files are stored only on the device where they were added and are never uploaded.

We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Account information

When you create an account, we collect your email address and a securely hashed password. We never store or see your plain-text password. This is used solely to identify your account and let you sign in.

2.2 Health-adjacent tracking information

The App lets you record information related to peptide protocols for yourself and anyone else in your household you manage, including peptide or substance names, vial and reconstitution details, doses, schedules, injection sites, administration records, symptoms, benefits, weight, body measurements, lab results, and related notes (“Health-Adjacent Data”).

This data is stored on your device. If you enable Cloud sync, structured tracking data is also stored using Supabase, a third-party hosted database and authentication provider. Supabase stores this data on our behalf so it can sync across your devices. We do not sell this data, and we do not share it with advertisers or unrelated third parties.

2.3 Optional sharing with another account

The App lets you optionally generate a time-limited invite code that grants another person’s separate HealthStix account read-only access to one specific profile’s protocol and dose history: what was taken, when, and how much. Symptoms, benefits, notes, body measurements, labs, and photos are never included in this shared view. You can revoke access at any time, which takes effect immediately. This is separate from syncing your own data across your devices.

2.4 Optional biometric authentication

If you enable fingerprint or Face ID unlock, it is handled entirely by your device’s operating system. Your biometric data never leaves your device and is never sent to us or Supabase. The App receives only a yes-or-no confirmation that authentication succeeded.

2.5 Subscription information

If you use HealthStix+, the App provides RevenueCat with your account email, display name, and account-scoped user identifier, and receives subscription-entitlement and purchase-history information needed to validate access, support your account, and restore purchases across supported devices. RevenueCat processes this information on our behalf. HealthStix and RevenueCat do not receive your payment-card or bank-account details; purchases are processed by Apple or Google.

2.6 Reminders and diagnostics

HealthStix reminders are scheduled locally by your device. The App does not currently register for remote push notifications or send a device push token to Expo. HealthStix does not currently include a third-party crash-reporting or analytics service.

2.7 App-store platform data

Apple and Google collect certain data independently of us as part of operating their app stores and devices. Their own privacy policies govern that collection.

3. How we use information

We use the information described above only to:

  • Operate core App functionality, including accounts, syncing tracking data across your devices, and sharing among profiles in your household.
  • Enable optional sign-in, authentication, and reminder features.
  • Validate HealthStix+ access and restore purchases.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell or rent it to any third party.

5. Data retention and deletion

Your account and tracking data are retained for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion at any time by contacting privacy@spacecityapps.com; we will delete applicable Supabase data within 30 days.

You can also delete your account and associated data from inside the App through Account & Settings → Delete Account. This immediately erases the App’s tracking data on your device and in Supabase and sends a request to remove the remaining authentication login record and RevenueCat customer profile, including account attributes and granted entitlements. We complete those removals within 30 days.

Deleting these records does not cancel an active subscription billed by Apple or Google. You must cancel that subscription separately through the applicable store. See the account-deletion instructions.

Progress-photo and vial-photo image files are stored only on your device, so deleting them or uninstalling the App removes them immediately. We never had a cloud copy.

6. Children’s privacy

This App is not directed at children and is not intended for anyone under age 16. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If HealthStix is used to help manage a household member’s protocol, the account holder is responsible for confirming that household member’s consent to being tracked in the App.

7. Your rights

You can view, edit, or delete your tracking data in the App. You can request a copy of your data or full account deletion by contacting privacy@spacecityapps.com. Depending on your location, you may have additional rights, including rights to access, correct, or delete personal information. Contact us to exercise those rights.

8. Medical disclaimer

HealthStix is a personal tracking and informational tool only. It is not a medical device, and it does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or dosing recommendations. Information in the peptide library is for general informational purposes only. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider regarding health-related decisions.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if, for example, we add a feature that changes what information is collected or how it is stored. We will update the “Last updated” date above when we do.

10. Contact us

Privacy questions and data requests can be sent to privacy@spacecityapps.com. For general HealthStix help, visit HealthStix support.
