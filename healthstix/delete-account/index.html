Delete your HealthStix account

You can permanently delete your account and associated data from inside HealthStix or request help by email.

Delete in the appRequest by emailWhat is deletedSubscriptions

Delete inside the app

  1. Open HealthStix and sign in.
  2. Open Account & Settings.
  3. Scroll to Delete Account.
  4. Read the warning and confirm permanent deletion.

This immediately erases HealthStix tracking data on that device and in Supabase. It also sends a request to remove the remaining authentication login record and RevenueCat customer profile, including account attributes and granted entitlements.

Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, email healthstixsupport@gmail.com from the email address associated with your HealthStix account. Use the subject “HealthStix account deletion request.” We may need to verify account ownership before acting.

We complete applicable authentication and RevenueCat removals within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your HealthStix authentication login record.
  • Structured cloud data associated with your account, including profiles, protocols, schedules, doses, effects, measurements, labs, and related records.
  • Your RevenueCat customer profile, account attributes, and granted entitlements associated with HealthStix.
  • Local HealthStix tracking data when deletion is completed inside the app.

Progress-photo and vial-photo image files remain only on the device where they were added. Deleting them, deleting the account in the app, clearing app storage, or uninstalling HealthStix removes the applicable local files; HealthStix does not hold a cloud copy.

Deleting an account does not cancel store billing

An active subscription billed through Apple or Google must be cancelled separately through that store. Deleting your HealthStix account does not cancel the store subscription.

Manage subscriptions through your Apple App Store or Google Play account before deleting HealthStix if you do not want future renewal charges.
